# Rooftop solar site survey checklist (day 1 on site)

Tick each item and record the value or a photo reference. If something is a "no go", stop and flag it to the design lead before leaving site.

## Before you go
- [ ] Client contact, site address, gate and safety induction time, who escorts you on the roof
- [ ] Latest electricity bills (12 months if possible): sanctioned or contract demand, connection voltage, tariff category, recorded demand, consumption, power factor
- [ ] Single-line diagram and as-built layout of the plant electrical room, if the client has them
- [ ] Roof drawings or structural details, if any
- [ ] PPE: helmet, safety shoes, harness with double lanyard, gloves, hi-vis, sunglasses, water
- [ ] Tools: laser distance meter, 30 m tape, compass or phone compass app, tilt meter or phone inclinometer, camera, multimeter, clamp meter, torch, marker, notebook, drone only if client and local rules allow

## 1. Roof
- [ ] Roof type: metal sheet, RCC slab, asbestos or fibre cement (asbestos is a hazard, stop and report), other
- [ ] Sheet profile, thickness, fastener type and spacing; sheet condition (rust, leaks, patches, brittle sheets)
- [ ] Purlin material, size and spacing; truss or rafter spacing; how old and how corroded
- [ ] For RCC: slab thickness, waterproofing condition, parapet height, headroom for stairs, drainage outlets
- [ ] Roof length, width (up the slope), tilt and azimuth of each slope; ridge direction
- [ ] Roof age and remaining life versus the 25-year plant life; any planned re-sheeting or repair
- [ ] Waterproofing: plan how fasteners or clamps will be sealed; no drilling through a sound RCC waterproof layer without approval
- [ ] Load-bearing: ask whether the structure can take about 15 to 25 kg per m2 of dead load plus wind uplift; any engineer report

## 2. Obstacles and shading
- [ ] Skylights and translucent sheets (position, width, fragile), ridge ventilators, exhaust fans, chimneys, AC condensers, water tanks, antennas, lightning arrestors
- [ ] Heights of all obstacles above the roof and distance from the planned array
- [ ] Neighbouring buildings, trees and poles that shade the roof; photos at roughly 9:00, 12:00 and 15:00 if possible
- [ ] Dust, smoke, chemical exhaust or other soiling sources nearby; bird roosting areas

## 3. Access and working at height
- [ ] Safe roof access: fixed ladder or stairs, ladder condition, roof hatch
- [ ] Walkways on the roof; fall protection: parapet, edge protection, lifeline anchor points
- [ ] Where the crane or hoist can stand and path for lifting modules; storage and laydown area
- [ ] Overhead power lines or other live hazards near the roof edge

## 4. Electrical room and connection
- [ ] Location of the LT or HT panel, main incomer, available breaker space for the solar feeder
- [ ] Transformer rating, voltage (11 kV / 0.415 kV), loading and age
- [ ] Meter type and location, who owns it, whether a net, bi-directional or generation meter is needed
- [ ] Earthing: existing earth pits, last measured resistance, space for new pits
- [ ] Space for inverters, ACDB and combiner boxes, ventilation and shade there
- [ ] Cable route from roof to electrical room (length, trays, trench, wall penetrations)
- [ ] Network reachable for monitoring (internet, mobile signal)

## 5. Safety and site conditions
- [ ] Site safety rules, permit to work procedure, working hours, restricted production zones
- [ ] Fire exits, fire extinguishers, hydrants, fire-access paths on the roof
- [ ] Lightning protection present and condition
- [ ] Flooding or waterlogging history, high wind or cyclone exposure, corrosive environment

## 6. Photos to take (label each)
- [ ] Whole building from each side; roof from the ground and from the roof
- [ ] Every roof slope with a person or tape for scale
- [ ] Each obstacle, the roof edge, the ridge, sheet and purlin close-ups
- [ ] Electrical room, panel, meter, transformer, earth pit, cable route
- [ ] Nameplates (transformer, meter, panel) and a recent electricity bill

## 7. Before you leave
- [ ] Go through the sketch with the client contact; agree anything restricted
- [ ] Back up photos and notes the same day; write down open questions
